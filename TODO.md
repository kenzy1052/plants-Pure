# Plants & Pure redesign outcomes

## Premium brand direction

- Replace the former orange-led palette with a red-led system based on the supplied brand imagery and a warm ivory / charcoal foundation.
- Use the supplied Plants & Pure logo and real company product photos; do not generate or introduce AI imagery.
- Create an ultra-modern, premium editorial feel with fine rules, spacious layouts, and restrained motion.

## Homepage and hero

- Replace the old collage hero with a coherent company-led hero that communicates “Healthy living on the go!” and shows real pouch/bottle products.
- Add animated section reveals and subtle product hover motion while respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Align home copy with the supplied company profile: Accra roots, quality, authenticity, trust, health & wellness, and innovation.

## Commerce experience

- Keep the existing catalogue/API, Supabase, cart, auth, checkout, Paystack and environment-variable boundaries unchanged.
- Make the homepage storefront lead naturally into the existing shop and product detail routes.
- Continue supporting live product data, search, filtering, add-to-cart, cart drawer, checkout and order tracking.

## Content and trust

- Surface the three product ranges: spices, herbs, and oils.
- Add quality proof and women’s entrepreneurship messaging from the company profile.
- Keep contact and story routes available for fuller company information.

## Immersive scroll-driven homepage hero — complete

- Replace the current flat home hero with a premium, cinematic, interactive product experience integrated into the existing Plants & Pure storefront; do not redesign the entire website, replace existing navigation, modify unrelated sections, remove functionality, or create a detached demo.
- Use a pure black (#000000) hero throughout, with no visible video frame, rectangular background, contrasting canvas, or background boundary; retain elegant serif/sans-serif typography and the existing brand identity.
- Stage 0 must show “HEALTHY LIVING ON THE GO.”, the large title “PLANTS & PURE”, two or three supplied packaged products centrally, “Nature’s goodness, carefully prepared for your kitchen.”, and a subtle “SCROLL TO EXPLORE ↓” cue.
- Stages 1–2 must transition smoothly from the opening composition: title fades/transforms, supplied products move toward the right with subtle 3D perspective, rotation, depth and parallax; avoid excessive spinning, bouncing, or basic slide-in effects.
- Stage 3 must settle the actual products on the right and show on the left “Quality spices, herbs and oils.”, the exact description “Wholesome, natural spices, herbs and oils from Accra, prepared with strict attention to quality, authenticity and trust.”, and working “Shop the collection” and “Find us in store” buttons.
- Stage 4 must release the pinned hero naturally into the existing website content, without an abrupt jump.
- Use only two or three authentic supplied product photographs (preferred: Cinnamon Powder, Garlic Powder, Curry Powder; alternatives are allowed); preserve actual packaging, labels, colours, proportions and branding. Remove source backgrounds cleanly where possible, maintain realistic edges, lighting, shadows and dimensionality, and never recreate, redesign, approximate, or replace the packaging with AI-generated substitutes. Do not add floating ingredients or generic stock imagery. Report a source-photo limitation rather than distorting a product.
- Pin the hero during the sequence; synchronize animation directly with scroll progress; allow backward scrolling to reverse it; use smooth interpolation without sudden stage jumps; release pinning at completion and restore normal page scrolling.
- Support desktop, tablet and mobile with responsive product placement, typography, perspective and duration; prioritize smooth rendering, efficient loading and image optimization; respect prefers-reduced-motion without sacrificing mobile usability.
